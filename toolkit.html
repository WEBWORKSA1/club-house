How to use these tools well

The dues calculator assumes some members won't pay — that's the single most common budgeting mistake new treasurers make. The agenda builder reserves time for welcomes and open discussion because meetings that are all business lose newcomers. The bylaws starter is a draft, not legal advice: if your club handles significant money, signs leases or applies for grants, check the requirements for associations or nonprofits where you operate.
