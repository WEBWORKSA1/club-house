Support us

Keep the clubhouse lights on

Club.House is free for every organizer. Reader support pays for operations, new tools, promotion of member clubs, hiring writers and funding contest prizes.

Where your money goes

40% Operations — hosting, tools, research

25% Promotion & marketing — getting member clubs discovered

20% Hiring talent — editors, creators, developers

15% Contests & prizes — grants for outstanding clubs

Supporter tiers

Member

$5/mo

Supporter badge & thanks in our letter.

Builder

$15/mo

Early access to new tools + vote on what we build next.

Patron

$50/mo

Name on our patrons wall + a featured listing for your club.

Club.House is not a registered charity; contributions are not tax-deductible. Organisations can also support us through sponsorship.
