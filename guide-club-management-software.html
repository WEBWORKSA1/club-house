How to Choose Club Management Software (2026 Buyer's Guide)

By Club.House Editorial · Updated October 30, 2026 · Related guides

What club management software does, what it costs, the features that matter and when you need it.

Club management software is a single system for member records, dues collection, event registration, communication and member self-service. For a 12-person book club, it's overkill. For a 200-member sports club collecting fees and running events, it can save a volunteer treasurer dozens of hours a year.

Do you actually need it? A quick test

You probably need dedicated software if three or more of these are true:

  • You have more than about 50 members.
  • You collect recurring dues or fees.
  • You run paid events or ticketing.
  • More than one volunteer needs access to the member list.
  • You spend more than two hours a month chasing renewals.
  • Members ask for a directory, portal or app.

If not, a shared spreadsheet, a form tool and a payment link will serve you well — and our free toolkit covers dues and agendas.

What it costs

Pricing usually falls into three models: free tiers with limited members or with ads; flat monthly plans that scale by contact count; and per-transaction fees layered on top of payment processing. For a small to mid-size club, expect anything from free to roughly the cost of a monthly venue booking. Always total the subscription plus payment fees for a realistic year before comparing.

Questions to ask every vendor

  1. Can we export all member and payment data at any time, on every plan?
  2. What are total fees on a typical dues payment?
  3. How many admin seats are included?
  4. Is there a free trial with our real data?
  5. Where is member data stored, and how is it protected?
  6. What happens to our data if we cancel?

Common mistakes

  • Buying for the club you hope to be instead of the one you are. Start simple; migrate later.
  • Ignoring export restrictions, which create lock-in.
  • Skipping volunteer training. The best tool fails if only one person knows how to use it.
