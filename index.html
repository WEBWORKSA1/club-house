FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Club.House free?

Yes. Guides, tools, the club finder and listings are free. We're supported by advertising, optional sponsorships and reader donations.

Are you the Clubhouse audio app?

No. Club.House is an independent resource for real-world and online clubs. We have no connection to the Clubhouse app or any company with a similar name. See our disclosure.

How does "Get Matched" work?

You tell us what your club needs. We review it personally and email you a short list of suitable providers. It's free; some providers may pay us a referral fee, which never changes your price.

Can I list my club?

Yes — submit it here. Standard listings are free; featured placement is available for clubs that want extra reach.
