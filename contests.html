Every month we celebrate a club making a real difference for its members or community. Free to enter — nominate your own club or one you love.

How winners are chosen

  • Impact (40%) — what difference does the club make?
  • Community (30%) — how welcoming and inclusive is it?
  • Creativity (20%) — fresh ideas, formats or events.
  • Story (10%) — how well the nomination tells it.

Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. A purchase does not improve your chances.
  2. Eligibility: nominators must be 18 or older. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Club.House staff and their households may not enter.
  3. Entry period: each monthly round runs from the first to the last day of the month (23:59 UTC).
  4. Judging: a panel scores entries using the published criteria. Decisions are final.
  5. Prizes: as listed for the round; grants are paid to the club's account or a designated officer. No cash alternative for non-cash prizes. Winners may be responsible for applicable taxes.
  6. Notification: winners are contacted by email and must respond within 14 days, or an alternate may be chosen.
  7. Publicity: by entering, you allow us to publish the club name, city and nomination story.
  8. Privacy: entry data is handled per our Privacy Policy.
  9. Sponsor: Club.House. Full terms are in our Terms of Use.
