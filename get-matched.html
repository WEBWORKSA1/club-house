FAQ

Why is this free?

Some providers pay us a referral fee if you choose them. That never increases your price, and we only recommend options we'd suggest to a friend.

Will I get spammed by sales calls?

No. We send you the shortlist; you decide who to contact. We don't pass your phone number on without your permission.

How long does it take?

Usually within 48 business hours. Urgent venue or insurance requests are prioritised.

Are you a provider ourselves?

No. Club.House is independent. We're a guide, not a vendor.
