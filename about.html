About

Clubs make life better. We make clubs easier.

Club.House is an independent publication for everyone who starts, runs or joins a club.

Research on wellbeing keeps reaching the same conclusion: strong relationships are among the best predictors of a long, healthy, happy life. Clubs are one of the simplest ways to build them — a regular time, a shared interest, familiar faces.

Yet starting and running a club is still harder than it should be. Organizers piece together advice from scattered PDFs, forums and vendor blogs. Would-be members wade through directories full of groups that stopped meeting years ago.

What we do

  • Find: a curated finder pointing to the most reliable club directories.
  • Start: step-by-step guides and a launch checklist.
  • Run: free tools for dues, agendas, bylaws and events.
  • Grow: playbooks for membership, fundraising and formats.
  • Connect: free matching with software, venues, insurance and sponsors.

Editorial principles

Our guides are written for organizers, not for vendors. We cite official sources where rules matter, update guides regularly, and label anything sponsored. Advertising and referral partnerships keep the site free, but never decide what we recommend.
