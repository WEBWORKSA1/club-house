Money

32 Club Fundraising Ideas That Actually Work

By Club.House Editorial · Updated October 30, 2026 · Related guides

Easy wins, signature events, sponsorships and recurring revenue ideas for clubs of every size.

The best fundraisers match your club's personality and use skills your members already have. Below are 32 ideas grouped by effort, from quick wins to signature events.

Quick wins (under a week to organize)

  1. Bake or snack sale at your regular meeting
  2. Pay-what-you-can open night
  3. Online donation link in every recap email
  4. Used-book, gear or kit swap with a small entry fee
  5. Restaurant give-back night (venue donates a share of sales)
  6. Raffle of donated items (check local raffle rules)
  7. "Round-up" option when members pay dues
  8. Sell digital photos from a club event

Member-powered ideas

  1. Skills workshop taught by a member
  2. Club-branded merch: shirts, mugs, tote bags
  3. Sponsored challenge — miles run, books read, games played
  4. Annual membership drive with a "founding supporter" tier
  5. Member talent show or showcase
  6. Club cookbook, zine or anthology
  7. Car wash or community service for donations
  8. Bring-a-friend guest-fee night

Signature events

  1. Annual tournament with entry fees
  2. Fun run or charity walk
  3. Quiz or trivia night
  4. Auction (silent or online)
  5. Gala dinner or anniversary party
  6. Film screening with discussion
  7. Community fair stall or open day
  8. Holiday market

Sponsorship & partnerships

  1. Local business event sponsor
  2. Season-long "presenting partner"
  3. In-kind sponsorship (venue, food, prizes)
  4. Newsletter sponsor slot
  5. Logo on shirts or banners
  6. Grant applications to community foundations
  7. Employer volunteer-matching programs
  8. Recurring supporter memberships

Pricing your event

Use the event ticket planner to set a price that covers costs and hits a profit goal after sponsorship. Price for the attendance you can realistically expect, not the attendance you hope for.

Stay on the right side of the rules

Raffles, lotteries and charitable solicitations are regulated in many places. Check local requirements, keep clear records, and tell donors honestly how funds will be used.
